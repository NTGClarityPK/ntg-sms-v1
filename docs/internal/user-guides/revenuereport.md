# Revenue report — user guide

A short guide for school administrators who view **collected income** across fee payments and ID card reprint fees.

---

## Who can use this?

- **School admin** or **principal** only  
- You must be **logged in** with a **branch selected** in the header  

**Where:** **Reports** → **Revenue** tab  

---

## What counts as revenue?

Only money **already collected** in the chosen period:

| Source | Included |
|--------|----------|
| **Fee collection** | Verified fee payments (by payment date) |
| **ID card reprints** | Reprint fees where a fee was recorded |

Pending or unpaid challans are **not** included. Operational fee reports (defaulters, overdue) stay under fee management, not this tab.

If your plan does not include fee management, you may still see ID card reprint revenue; fee totals show as not on your plan.

---

## Filters

**Period** — Week, month, or year (sets start and end dates).

**Branch scope** (if you have more than one branch):

- **Current branch** — header branch only  
- **All branches** — every branch you can access  
- **Choose branch** — one specific branch  

---

## Summary vs detailed report

Use the **Detailed report** switch:

| View | Shows |
|------|--------|
| **Off (summary)** | Grand total, totals per category, branch breakdown, fee totals by payment method (e.g. bank transfer, cash) |
| **On (detailed)** | Same totals plus **each payment**: student name, challan, method, date, amount; and **each ID card reprint fee**: name, type, card number, date, amount |

---

## Export

**Export** → **PDF** or **Excel**. The file matches your current period, branch scope, and summary/detailed setting. PDFs include your **school name**, **logo**, and **branch** (or “all branches”) at the top.

---

## Quick tips

- Switch branch in the header, then use **Current branch** to match that branch.  
- Use **All branches** for a school-wide picture across campuses.  
- Turn on **Detailed report** before export if you need a line-by-line audit.
