# 📞 Support

## Before You Contact Support

1. Check the relevant [feature guide](../features/README.md)
2. Review [🆘 Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md)
3. Confirm the branch and academic year selected in the portal
4. Try refreshing the page or signing in again

## Who to Contact

| Situation | Contact |
| --- | --- |
| Access or permission problems | Your school administrator |
| Incorrect student, staff, or class data | Your school administrator |
| Portal errors or unexpected behaviour | Your school administrator, who escalates to NTG support |
| Billing and subscription questions | Your school administrator or NTG support |

## What to Include in a Report

* School and branch name
* Your role
* The page you were on
* Approximate date and time
* What you expected to happen
* What actually happened
* A screenshot, where possible

## 🔄 Documentation Feedback

If a guide is unclear, missing, or out of date, tell the product team so the page can be corrected — documentation is maintained alongside the product.
