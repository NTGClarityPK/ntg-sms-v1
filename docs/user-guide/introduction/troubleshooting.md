# 🆘 Troubleshooting

## Common Issues

**❌ Cannot Login:**

* Verify your email and password
* Check your internet connection
* Use **Forgot password** to reset
* Contact your school administrator if the account is locked

**🙈 Menu Item Missing:**

* Your role may not have permission for that module
* The module may not be included in your school's plan
* Confirm the branch selected in the header

**🏢 Wrong School Data Showing:**

* Confirm the branch shown in the header
* Confirm the active academic year
* Refresh the page after switching branch

**🌐 Portal in the Wrong Language:**

* Open the language button in the top bar and check which option is ticked
* If **Use school default** is ticked, the language comes from Settings → Business Information
* Ask an admin to change the school default if needed

**📄 Page Empty After Login:**

* Confirm an academic year is **Active**
* Confirm you have a role assigned on that branch
* Confirm academic structure (classes and sections) exists

**📥 PDF Not Downloading:**

* Disable the browser pop-up blocker
* Try a different browser
* Confirm the required records exist for that student or class

**🔔 Not Receiving Notifications:**

* Check the notification bell for unread items
* Allow browser notifications if prompted
* Confirm your contact details are correct on your profile

**💾 Changes Not Appearing Elsewhere:**

* Refresh the page
* Confirm you saved the change
* Confirm you are in the correct branch context
